The internet has become obsessed with one question. Was AI involved?

But what happens after we've answered it? Once disclosure has happened, the interesting questions aren't about the tool anymore. They're about the work.

> From detection to disclosure. From disclosure to explanation. From explanation to accountability. And eventually, back to evaluating the work itself.

The value was never in proving that AI wasn't used. The value was always in demonstrating that the work deserved to be trusted.

Stop chasing the tool. Start evaluating the thinking.
